**Sacroiliitis** means swelling or irritation in one or both sacroiliac (SI) joints. These joints sit at the bottom of your spine, where it meets your pelvis. They help carry the weight of your upper body when you stand and walk.

When these joints are irritated, you may feel pain in your low back or buttock. It can feel like other back problems, so a careful exam is important. Our doctors work to find the true cause and plan care that fits you.
