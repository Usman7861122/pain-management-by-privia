**Personal injury pain** is pain that starts after an accident or injury, such as a car crash, a fall or an injury at work. It can come from muscles, joints, the spine or nerves.

Some people feel better in a few weeks. For others, pain stays. Our team helps you understand the cause, treats your pain, and keeps careful records of your care.
