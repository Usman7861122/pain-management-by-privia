**Neuropathy** means damage to the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord. These nerves carry feeling and movement signals around the body. When they are hurt, the signals can be wrong or weak.

This can cause burning, tingling, numbness or pain. Many people feel it first in the feet or hands. It can be hard to live with, but there are ways to help.
