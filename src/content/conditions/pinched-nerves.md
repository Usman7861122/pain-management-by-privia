A **pinched nerve** happens when bone, a disc, muscle or swelling presses on a nerve. The pressure can make the nerve irritated and stop it from working well.

Pinched nerves can happen in the neck, back, arms or legs. The pain may stay in one place or travel along the nerve. Finding the exact source is the first step to good care.
