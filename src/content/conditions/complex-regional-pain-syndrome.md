**Complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS)** is a rare condition that causes long-lasting pain, usually in one arm, hand, leg or foot. The pain is often much stronger than the original injury, and it may not go away as the injury heals.

CRPS can also cause swelling, skin changes and stiffness. It needs careful, expert care. The sooner it is found and treated, the better the chance of keeping your limb moving.
