**Cancer pain** is pain that comes from cancer, or from the treatment for cancer. It can be mild or strong. It can come and go, or stay all day.

Pain is not something you must accept. We work alongside your cancer care team to find the cause and ease your pain, so you can feel more comfortable and rest better.
