**Sciatica** is pain along the sciatic nerve. This nerve starts in the lower back and runs through the buttock and down each leg. When it is irritated or pinched, pain can travel along its path.

Sciatica is a symptom, not a disease. Finding what presses on the nerve is the key to good treatment.
