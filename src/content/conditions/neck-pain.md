Neck pain is pain or stiffness in the area from the base of your head to your shoulders. It can be mild or strong. It can be short or long lasting.

Sometimes the problem is in the **cervical spine**, the bones and discs of the neck. A pinched nerve there can cause pain in the arm as well.
