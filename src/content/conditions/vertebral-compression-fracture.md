A **vertebral compression fracture** happens when one of the bones of the spine (a vertebra) cracks and collapses. The bone becomes flatter on one side. This can cause sudden back pain and can change the shape of your spine.

These fractures are common in people with weak bones. They can also happen after an injury. Early care can help you control pain and stay active, so please tell us about new back pain soon.
