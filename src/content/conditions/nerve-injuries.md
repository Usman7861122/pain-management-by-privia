A **nerve injury** happens when a nerve is stretched, pressed, cut or irritated. Nerves carry signals between the brain and the body. When a nerve is hurt, those signals can change.

This can cause pain, tingling, numbness or weakness. Some nerve injuries heal with time. Others cause pain that lasts, and a pain specialist can help you manage it.
