Between the bones of your spine are soft cushions called **discs**. A disc has a tough outer wall and a soft center. In a **herniated disc**, some of the soft center pushes out through the wall.

The bulge can press on a nearby nerve. It can also cause swelling around the nerve. This can lead to pain, numbness or weakness.
