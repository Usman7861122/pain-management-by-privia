The bones of your spine form a tunnel called the **spinal canal**. The spinal cord and nerves pass through it. In **spinal stenosis**, this space becomes narrow.

A narrow space can squeeze the nerves. This can cause pain, numbness or weakness in the back, neck, arms or legs.
