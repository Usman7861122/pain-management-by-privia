Neuromodulation uses electrical stimulation to change how nerves work. The types used for chronic pain include spinal cord stimulation, peripheral nerve stimulation, HFX therapy, SmartLoop therapy and dorsal root ganglion (DRG) stimulation. A device is placed under the skin to deliver the stimulation.

It may be an option for nerve-damage pain when other care has not worked. Our physicians are experienced with both trials and implants, so one team guides you from your first exam through surgery and long-term care.
