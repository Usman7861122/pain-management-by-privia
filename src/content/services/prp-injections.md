**Platelet-rich plasma (PRP)** is made from a small sample of your own blood. Platelets are blood cells that help the body heal. In PRP they are gathered in a higher amount and placed in an injured or worn joint or tendon.

PRP is often used for the pain of arthritis and for tendon injuries. It is a good choice for people who want an option that uses their own body's healing factors.
