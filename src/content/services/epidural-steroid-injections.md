An epidural steroid injection places anti-inflammatory medicine into the epidural space, the area around the spinal cord. The medicine reaches the spot where pinched or irritated nerves leave the spine.

It is used for severe, long-lasting back or neck pain that spreads into the arms or legs. When a nerve is pinched or irritated this way, it is called **radiculopathy**. It can cause pain, weakness, numbness, and burning or tingling.
