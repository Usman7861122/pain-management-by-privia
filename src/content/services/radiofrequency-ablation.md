Radiofrequency ablation, also called **rhizotomy**, uses radiofrequency energy to interrupt the nerves that carry pain signals from arthritic joints to the brain. The goal is long-term relief.

Dr. Stuart Hough and Dr. Ramani Peruvemba are fellowship-trained in this procedure. They have performed it many times and have decades of experience.

Think of pruning a bush: the nerve is trimmed, not removed, so the pain signal is cut while the nerve can regrow over time.
