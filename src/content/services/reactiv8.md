Some people develop chronic low back pain after an injury because of **neuromuscular inhibition**. Pain leads to muscle disuse, weakness and an unstable spine, which leads to more pain. Exams and MRIs often show that the muscles along the spine (the multifidus muscles) have weakened and shrunk, and physical therapy alone does not fix it.

Reactiv8 is an implanted device that uses mild electrical pulses to strengthen these muscles. It is described as the first "restorative" stimulator for pain. Spinal cord and nerve stimulators change how the nervous system reads pain signals. Reactiv8 instead treats the muscle problem underneath.

Few interventional pain practices in the area are trained to evaluate and treat patients with Reactiv8.
