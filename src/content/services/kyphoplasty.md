Balloon kyphoplasty treats back pain caused by a broken vertebra. Bone cement stabilizes the fracture, and the balloon can restore lost height and ease the curve in the spine. It has been used since the 1990s, mostly for fractures from osteoporosis and cancer.

Osteoporosis fractures are a common source of spine pain in older adults. About one in three women and one in eight men over 50 have osteoporosis.

Dr. Stuart Hough and Dr. Ramani Peruvemba are fellowship-trained and offer kyphoplasty at both offices. Few local pain practices are trained to do it.
