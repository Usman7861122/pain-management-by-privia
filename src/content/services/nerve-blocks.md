A nerve block places numbing medicine near a nerve to relieve pain from a pinched or damaged nerve. It can also show us which nerve is sending the pain signals.

We treat nerve pain in the neck, back of the head, upper back, lower back, chest, abdomen, wrist (carpal tunnel) and leg. Physical therapy, chiropractic care and medicine are usually tried first. If pain does not improve, a nerve block can become an important part of your plan.
