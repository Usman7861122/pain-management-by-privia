iovera® is a short outpatient procedure called **percutaneous cryoneurolysis**. It uses extreme cold to temporarily interrupt the sensory nerves that carry pain from areas such as the knee. It needs no general anesthesia and no cuts.

It is most often used before knee replacement surgery, to reduce pain and the need for opioids for up to three months afterward. Dr. Stuart Hough and Dr. Ramani Peruvemba have fellowship training in iovera®, and few local practices offer it.
