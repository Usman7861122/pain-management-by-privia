Dr. Peruvemba grew up in the New York City area. He studied at Washington University in St. Louis and New York Medical College.

He trained in anesthesiology at New York Presbyterian Hospital and completed a fellowship in interventional pain management at Mt. Sinai Hospital in New York.

He joined Pain Management Specialists in 2000. He treats complex regional pain syndrome and uses spinal cord stimulation and spinal injections to help patients move with less pain.
