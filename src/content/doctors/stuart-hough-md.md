Dr. Hough studied at Swarthmore College and the University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry. He then trained in anesthesiology and pain management at Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston.

After training, he served three years in the U.S. Air Force. He came back to the Washington area in 2001 and has cared for patients here ever since.

Today he leads the Division of Pain Management at Shady Grove Medical Center. His focus includes cancer pain, spinal cord stimulation, and spinal and ultrasound-guided injections.
